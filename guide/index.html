Getting there

Quartier Papier, Fabriekstraat 55-59, 1930 Zaventem.

  • By train: direct trains from Brussels-Schuman, Central and Midi to Zaventem take about 15 minutes. The venue is a 10-minute walk from Zaventem station.
  • From the airport: 5 minutes by taxi from Brussels Airport.
  • By car: free parking with EV charging at the venue.

Running late? The registration desk stays staffed, so just come in. You can also find a volunteer or call or text +49 157 57123553.

Arrival & registration

Enter at Fabriekstraat 55-59. The registration desk is on the ground floor, where you'll pick up your name badge. You can leave your coat and luggage at the cloakroom next to it.

The talks and panel are in Satin, on the 3rd floor. Dinner is in Soie, on the ground floor. There's a lift to all floors.

Food & drinks

Coffee, tea and water are available all afternoon, starting at 13:30. There are sandwiches and pastries when you arrive, and snacks in each break. Dinner is a buffet from 19:30, catered by Artfood, with wine, beer and non-alcoholic drinks. Menu cards at the buffet list the allergens for each dish.

The exact menu will be available a few days before the event. Roughly:

  • Arrival (13:30): sandwiches, for anyone who hasn't had lunch
  • First break: cheesecake, carrot cake and fresh fruit salad
  • Last break: sharing boards and finger sandwiches
  • Dinner (19:30): cold and warm buffet, including vegan dishes

We've passed on the dietary needs from your registration to the caterer. If anything changes, just email us at mail@aisafetyaachen.org and we'll do our best.

Chatham House Rule, photos & recordings

The Chatham House Rule applies by default, unless a speaker says otherwise. You're free to share what you hear at the Forum, but please don't reveal who said it or which organisation they're from.

We're planning to have a journalist from DIE ZEIT attend the Forum, following the Chatham House Rule like everyone else. If you'd like to give an interview, we'll be happy to arrange one for you in a separate room.

We won't take photos or recordings ourselves. Photos and recordings of people are only allowed with their consent.

Accessibility

All floors are reachable by lift. If you have any accessibility needs, let us know in advance and we'll make sure they're covered.

Getting help

Volunteers in team T-shirts are around all day, and the registration desk on the ground floor is staffed throughout. For anything urgent on the day, call or text +49 157 57123553. If you need a quiet spot for a call, ask a volunteer.

Before the event, email mail@aisafetyaachen.org.

Getting home & after the event

  • Trains from Zaventem back to Brussels run into the late evening; check the SNCB app for times. Volunteers are happy to help you call a taxi.
  • If we agreed to cover your travel, send your receipts and bank details to mail@aisafetyaachen.org after the event.
  • If you have any feedback after the Forum, we'd love to hear it at mail@aisafetyaachen.org.

Privacy

We use your registration details only to organise the Forum: the guest list on the event page, badges, catering and travel reimbursement. Ask us at mail@aisafetyaachen.org if you'd like anything changed or removed.
