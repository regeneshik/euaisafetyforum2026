Venue & Arrival

Quartier Papier, Fabriekstraat 55-59, 1930 Zaventem (Brussels). Registration opens at 13:30. You'll get a name badge at the desk, and volunteers will be around to help.

  • 10-minute walk from Zaventem station, 15 minutes by direct train from Brussels-Schuman, Central and Midi
  • 5 minutes by taxi from Brussels Airport
  • Free parking with EV charging. If driving, use Waze: local traffic filters confuse standard GPS
